## Source properties

**Original Source** is how the contact first arrived in HubSpot. It never changes.
**Latest Source** is how they most recently came back. It updates with each new visit.

"Offline Sources" just means they arrived through something other than a website visit: an import, a manual creation, an integration, or Apollo. It doesn't mean the data is wrong.

## Asking for a new property

Check this guide first, since most "new property" requests are already covered. If it isn't, raise it with [Gerard] and include: what you want to record, who fills it in, and what report or list it's for. Properties without a reporting use don't get created.
