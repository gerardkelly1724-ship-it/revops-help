## Pipelines

| Pipeline | Use for |
|---|---|
| New Business | First contract with an institution or business |
| Renewals | Renewing an existing contract. CS owns these. |
| Partnerships | Deals sourced or resold through partners |

If a deal is in the wrong pipeline, you can move it yourself from the deal record. The stage will reset, so pick the equivalent stage in the new pipeline.

## New Business stages and exit criteria

| Stage | Move on when |
|---|---|
| Discovery | Need confirmed and a meeting with the budget holder is booked |
| Demo Completed | Demo delivered to the decision makers |
| Proposal Sent | Written proposal sent with pricing and a target start date |
| Procurement | Institution has started its procurement or approval process |
| Closed Won | Signed contract or PO received |
| Closed Lost | Lost or stalled for [90] days with no response |

## Common questions

**Renewal: new deal or update the old one?** New deal in the Renewals pipeline. Never reopen a closed won deal, it breaks revenue reporting.

**Close Reason on closed lost deals.** Required. HubSpot won't let you close the deal without it. Pick the closest reason and add detail in the notes.

**Amount on a multi-year deal.** Put the first-year value in *Amount* and the total contract value in *Total Contract Value*. Reports and forecasts use *Amount*.
